The inspiration

Why I built this

We love sharing proof that we’re healthy.

A run. A pace. A step count.

But one thing shaping our health never shows up in those posts: the air we breathe.

Air is invisible, so it’s easy to forget.

I wanted to make it visible, inside the photos we already take and share.

Aera stamps every photo with the air where it was taken, the way Strava stamps a run.

A clean-air day becomes something to show off. A bad one becomes something people notice.

A question I keep coming back to:

What would change if we could see the air we breathe?
